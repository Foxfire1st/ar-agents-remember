## Governing Overview

[Overview](../../overview.md)

## Purpose

Measures the current instruction corpus and checks its authored capsule, detail and digest ceilings.

## Code Commentary

Measurements cover the capsule matrix, lifecycle sources and applicable detail and template populations, with source identities and compiler revisions. Current size deltas are reported from these measurements. A measured source size is not an approval or an individual item ceiling. Ordinary templates are measured without a detail ceiling; authored role, detail and digest ceilings decide excessive growth.

## Relationships

The planner receives prospective generated facts before measuring final current carriers. The current budget document keeps authored decisions separate from its generated measurements.
