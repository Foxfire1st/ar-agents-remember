## Purpose and current source account

The scrub removes AR/Paseo/source/former-host prefixes and exact Python/Git/caller-session names, then installs four sandbox-owned values; each child receives its own PWD. Credentials and harness home selectors remain. Running daemon admission compares scrubbed selectors and expected owned values, and the Eve env denylist prevents reintroduction by its external key file.
