# Held-seat launcher word

[Dashboard source overview](../../overview.md)

## Contract

The rendered launcher fixture labels a bound held Worker “Worker (held)”, not running, and disables its ordinary Start; a running Worker retains its running label. The fixture is confined to the word/status surface. It neither drives replacement nor proves backend holder authority or real-host execution.

## Evidence


- A held bound seat is named held and its ordinary Start is disabled. [1]


- Running naming remains distinct from held naming. [2]
