## Purpose

Card-excerpt marker controls preserve owner hunk identity, occurrence navigation and explicit pending, unreadable and other-content states.

## Code Commentary

### Logic

- Real retained MIK-L32 classification bodies and their exact fixture blob text are wrapped in synthetic per-entry cards and a plain marker-scope value. A card may contain the whole fixture text, but there is no card-owned Full file mode and no source-content fetch.
- The split fixture keeps three separate owner hunks, including its deletion in the before editor; inline puts the marks in the one editor. Each mark retains its compact label, full accessible name, exact line spans and exposed control gutter while ordinary line numbers remain hidden. The scope classifies the file exactly once.
- A hunk list preserves two intents, their four distinct family occurrence states, the explicit intersection-only disclaimer and exact block-pane/family/member-revision follow arguments. A proof stays in a separate tests group with its facet, and a readable side keeps its marks when the opposite knowledge side is unavailable.
- Confirmed-unregistered and both-unread files get file-level notes without hunk marks. A blob mismatch on either drawn side suppresses marks with other-content. Partial inventory absence explains unlisted without a read; a complete inventory absence has nothing to mark.
- Returning to the exact excerpt pane reopens its list, scrolls and focuses the named mark. A fake-frame settling hold restores a blur until the reader presses a key; a different originating pane neither reopens nor settles.
- Unchanged-range, changed-diff and separate-side excerpts cross controlled classification promises with ready and unavailable answers. Each shows loading before resolution, no premature mark, one classification call and the appropriate final marks or exact failure note. A genuinely unchanged file asks nothing and has no state note.

### Invariants And Boundaries

Classification capture provenance is an input qualification, not a new producer run. Scope methods, return geometry and animation frames are controlled. The suite was not executed here and establishes no live API, installed browser or phone-width layout result.
