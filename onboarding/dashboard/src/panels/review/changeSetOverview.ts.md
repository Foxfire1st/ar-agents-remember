## Governing Overview

[Route overview](../overview.md)

## Purpose

A subject's summary as the reading column shows it.

## Code Commentary

A summary is complete or absent, never a partial one presented as whole: without a ready overview there is no summary, and where entries exist the counts and the weakest currentness come from the entries themselves rather than from a default.

## Boundaries

The obligation above is the whole of what this file must keep true; the route account says what the surrounding surface promises.

## Evidence

- No ready overview means no summary at all, rather than a summary of zeroes that reads as measured. [1]

- A subject's currentness is the weakest of its entries, so a summary cannot claim better than its parts. [2]
