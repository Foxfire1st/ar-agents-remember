## Purpose

The shared L86 mounted-test world: exact leaf-built L37 responses served per route with asked-URL capture and optional held lane or worklist replies, over the recorded catalogue, family, member, task, trees, cards, lane, change-set and diff captures.

## Code Commentary

Captured helpers load each frozen answer by name with source URLs and hashes in the provenance capture, and the target names the recorded L37 history. Intent replies distinguish member from family and task subjects; tree replies distinguish classification, lane, inventory and member-versus-family cards by query. Held replies suspend lane or worklist answers until released, and unmapped routes throw instead of serving a guess.
