# dashboard/src/panels/review/ReviewWorkspace.family.test.tsx

## Governing Overview

[Owning route overview](overview.md)

## Purpose

Family-review controls preserve exact authored operands and bounded roster state while placing selected content before supporting details.

## Code Commentary

### Logic

- Selector-keyed retained tree bodies answer only their own family/member requests. Direct FamilyReviewCenter controls explicitly project invariant-selector family context; the separate synthetic catalogue and composed summary do not become captured producer evidence.
- The selected family reconstructs exact before/after guarantee and full member statements from the rendered word diffs. Opening a member reads its owner, retains its family and keeps guarantee, statement, membership, source and authored judgment facts separate. The one All changes action and workspace survive family selection.
- The selected reading order is meta line, guarantee, wording-layout control, recorded steps and judgment, then member sections and finally the roster. State sentences, origin and raw revision IDs remain in closed Technical details; fully read, task-written content has no redundant family/carried chip.
- A partial-context chip carries the exact remaining/total count and continues with that roster cursor. Synthetic writer overlays distinguish recorded outside writer, no recorded writer, unavailable history and no carried writer; a revision the reviewed task wrote has no carried chip.
- Bounded, intentionally uncarried, content_not_on_page, before-only, one-sided/not_recorded, measured-zero, final-walk and whole-selection cases retain separate words. Family continuation sends the published cursor and rejects another walk while keeping the coherent answer. Omitted family context is a short unread chip plus its detailed sentence, never measured empty.
- Two same-subject continuations preserve the workspace and filter and issue their exact cursors. Source-address deduplication collapses five changed rows to four addresses without losing the five membership occurrences; it does not claim that these addresses are the measured change set.
- The whole view has at most twenty details, restricted to identifiers/provenance, lists every supplied row/proof identifier and leaves their judgment text outside details. Synthetic growth to nine rows and fifty-seven proofs leaves the disclosure count unchanged.

### Invariants And Boundaries

Captured operands, synthetic catalogue/summary, direct roster projection and explicitly edited writer/omission/growth probes qualify only these declared component behaviors. Historical full-tree data isolates chrome. No tests, native producer or real 390px/browser geometry were executed here.
