## Purpose

The layout Curators copy for the onboarding overview of one cohesive source area.

## Code Commentary

A leading comment sends the Curator to the card-and-overview scope rules in `reference/curator/card-formats-and-citations.md`. Sections are Governing Overview (link to the parent overview), What This Area Is, Hot Path Summary, Ownership and Neighbours, Structures and Operating Model, Main Flows, Load-Bearing Files (with a Coverage column: current or deferred with reason), Contracts and Traps, Relationships and Documentation, Child Overviews (route and link), Using This Area and Open Questions, and an Evidence table of Finding | Anchor | Source.

## Evidence

- The area overview links its parent overview and explains the area in its own right. [1]
- Load-bearing files carry a coverage column, and child overviews are listed by route. [2]
- Evidence is a Finding, Anchor and Source table. [3]
