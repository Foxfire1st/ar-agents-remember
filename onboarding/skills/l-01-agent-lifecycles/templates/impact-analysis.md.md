## Purpose

The canonical impact-analysis template defines a durable integrity-bulwark report for orchestrator portfolio analysis and the adversarial reviewer's completion/code-quality lenses. It makes both planned-versus-planned collisions and planned-versus-past regressions visible. The packaged runtime file is its distribution twin, not another report-contract authoring home.

## Code Commentary

### Logic

The report records recipient, analytical author label, subject and written time, followed by the surface swept, routes/areas, code anchors, both risk axes, evidence inventory and a bottom line for the spawning agent's main loop. Planned-versus-planned includes present or future master/leaf collisions and suggested resolutions such as foundation extraction, leaf movement or sequencing. Planned-versus-past names already-landed work at risk and its evidence limits.

Evidence-first findings cite route indexes and the backing `read_ar_files` reads. The inventory specifically records read_ar_files namings (IDs and cards, then natively opened). Naming locates the material to inspect; it is not source/onboarding text or independent proof of a collision or regression. Each finding states what its evidence does not prove.

### Conventions

The author field is an analysis role or bounded fan-out label, not a field specified for a runtime occupant ID. Keep analytical responsibility distinct from transport identity. Maintain the canonical template as the contract; preserve the inherited package-sync intent without treating source byte equality as proof that synchronization or installation ran.

### Invariants And Boundaries

This is a report, not a decision. Its bottom line feeds orchestrator reshape/spirit-test recommendations or reviewer judgment; the template says it never decides and performs no mutation. A fan-out author does not obtain an AR seat or boundary authority by writing the report. The current router keeps task boundary acts with the owning seat, and the current Orchestrator distinguishes delivery reports, findings, review, curation and Git publication rather than inferring acceptance from host status.

The inherited portfolio-integrity and shared-frame notes preserve the separation between analytical artifacts and the spawning seat's mutation authority. Their older role headings, line ranges and artifact-obligation phrasing are historical source locators, not an additional current role-launch or permission rule. Current role and operation bindings, rather than this template's recipient field, determine what a seat may do.

### Documentation Scope

The inherited card recorded no template-local TODO and required no sibling-repository evidence for this report contract. Those bounded statements do not establish a whole-repository absence or fresh external-reference survey.
