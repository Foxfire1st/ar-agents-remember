## Purpose and handover boundary

One schema serves profile-fit role takeover, Worker respawn and master-complete handover. The successor must act from the packet alone rather than a prior transcript. It states the agreed request, decisions, constraints, canonical task/report links, open questions and current committed/uncommitted state. A takeover records the profile mismatch without making profile settings the packet author’s operator choice. The shape carries from/to seat and canonical task identities; it grants no new role or write authority.

## Message/file transport

This canonical template requires messages to be short wake-ups naming requested work or a handover and its files. Report, verdict, list and evidence contents travel through file-only `body` or existing-file `attach`, not pasted into message text. Within a shared task, files stay with its record and a leaf workspace exposes the message-folder link. Text uses the effective settings limit without a pinned number. The canonical source and generated copies share this contract; transport does not change their existing write/start powers, peer destinations or independent acceptance authority.


- Packet-only recovery and file-carried handover are the template’s explicit rules; wake text uses settings without a pinned number. [1]
