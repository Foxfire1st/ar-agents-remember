## Purpose

This delivered detail names the curation step for each section. Reconciliation compares current intent, ruled intent and implementation and surfaces divergences. Before ingest, each target needs authored rationale (and optional role), the serving writer's report state/individual refusals are consumed, and invariant scope carries applicability, conditions and exclusions.

Family coverage is explicitly authored from justified joint obligations, never grouping by directory, and membership prompts a guarantee examination. Route changed files/notes to their actual cards/overviews/entity owner; Operational Notes is last resort, indexes remain generated, and overview/task-log dumping is forbidden. Readback and reporting keep authored/updated and examined-unchanged families distinct.

The file also preserves test-green versus intent-green, causal applicability/reconsideration of historical oddities, immutable baseline/candidate comparison and real rejected alternatives. Command/format detail is delegated to named workspace-delivered files at its step. The instruction’s repetition of the card-detail read is a source feature, not independent evidence of a read or a new writer lane. The whole result, role inputs and all-stage boundaries remain in the role/operation capsule.
