## Governing Overview

[Lifecycle overview](../overview.md)

## Purpose

Give Curators and Investigators one bounded procedure for researching a repository's first or resumed knowledge foundation: review the developer-facing source inventory, scout before area research, keep resumable progress and open questions, and treat a registry entry or search hit as a pointer rather than proof.

## Code Commentary

The detail tells the reader to resolve the repository context and the memory's `system/sources.md` registry before selecting a source, to show the developer the inventory (source, category, location, status, planned use; excluded sources and their reasons; weak categories) before asking for additions, and to record the developer's corrections with the inventory in the task notes. Research is bounded: a shallow scout assigns every eligible top-level path to a functional area or excludes it, records a technology profile, cross-repository interface map, deferred areas and open questions, and names one concrete action that resumes the work. Evidence rules require opening the document behind a registry entry or search hit, recording negative results, checking the branch of a neighbouring repository, and citing it in the converter's form. Research writes evidence in the task's notes and writes no memory. The detail also carries the inventory table shape and states that the procedure's source inventory is the single place the foundation detail reads for that account.

## Evidence

- The source inventory is shown and corrected before research begins. [1]
- Research scouts shallowly, assigns areas, and records resumable progress and open questions. [2]
- A registry entry or search hit is a pointer, not proof; research writes no memory. [3]
