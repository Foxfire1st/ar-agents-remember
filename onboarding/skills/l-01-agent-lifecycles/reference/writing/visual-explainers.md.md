## Purpose

Guidance for authoring an optional visual explainer or walkthrough: an application of the shared technical-English writing profile, not a second style guide.

## Code Commentary

A walkthrough is bound to the exact candidate and cites the invariant revision or family guarantee it explains, reusing existing term records and keeping identifiers, quoted source text and admitted effect labels exact. The promise, its actor and trigger, and its conditions and exclusions are shown together; a diagram must not hide a condition in an unlinked legend. Promise, realization and proof stay separate, an arrow establishes no test result, real evidence is linked with its scope and limitations, and missing, unexamined, unavailable and measured absence are not rendered as one empty success state. A family walkthrough explains the authored joint guarantee, not a grouping inferred from its members.

A paraphrase keeps the exact recorded text beside or behind an explicit link and goes to the profile's independent meaning review with conforming, violating and boundary cases; an ambiguous explanation stays unaccepted until corrected or classified through the normal writer/history/revision route, and frozen records or closed history are never edited to match art. An optional walkthrough supplements the required report and evidence and replaces no schema, admission or invariant check, independent review or acceptance record. The manifest lists the Worker as reader, and the Worker role points here before adding an optional walkthrough to its freeze report.
