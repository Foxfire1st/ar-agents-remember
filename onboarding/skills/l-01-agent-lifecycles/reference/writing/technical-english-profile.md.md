[overview](../../overview.md)

## Purpose

The one shared writing profile, inspired by Simplified Technical English, that Curators and explainer authors use for invariant statements, family guarantees, history reasons and walkthrough prose. It selects ASD-STE100 clarity principles and claims neither full compliance, a measured percentage nor a new controlled-language standard.

## Code Commentary

A promise names its actor with concrete verbs, states one independently testable obligation per invariant and one idea per paragraph, and normally targets about 20–25 words with precision first; an invariant is never split automatically to meet a word target, because splitting is an authored semantic change. Conditions, exceptions, quantifiers and negation are explicit, and one concept keeps one term. The profile orients the author on invariant, family, joint guarantee, realization, proof, history row, revision and candidate using existing term records and creates no other terminology registry. Identifiers, quoted source text and admitted effect labels stay exact, and missing, unexamined, unavailable and measured absence are distinct evidence states, none of which proves equivalence.

The statement and its applicability, conditions and exclusions stay in their own semantic fields and are read together; realizations, proofs, identities and provenance keep their structural owners, and a history reason explains the concrete change and its consequence. Before publication, a rewrite goes through the existing independent fact-check: the old revision and candidate are frozen, actor, trigger, required and forbidden behaviour, quantifiers, conditions and exclusions are compared facet by facet, and a non-author reader checks conforming, violating and boundary cases. `clarify` is proposed only after independent review establishes the same obligation; a real change uses the existing effect and writer/history/revision route, and disputed or ambiguous drafts stay unaccepted. A worked landing example with case and draft-fault tables illustrates the comparison and is not pilot evidence.

The profile applies to new or revised statements, guarantees, history reasons and walkthrough prose; it does not justify running the writer to demonstrate style, rewriting frozen history, backfilling closed rows or bulk-rewriting records, and grants no scratch-clone or writer authorization. It is guidance only: no automatic semantic validator, no word-count or vocabulary approval, no language gate and no closeout bypass. The Curator role and the hand-off list template point here before drafting statements, guarantees or history reasons, and `visual-explainers.md` applies it to walkthroughs.
