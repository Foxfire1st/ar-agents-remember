## Purpose

Master-exit accounts for every requirement, leaf, substep and accepted blank-fill. Skipped/reshaped work has a decision-log trail; unfinished work cannot hide in a complete-looking packet. Silent absence is a blocking finding. This detailed check does not turn the reviewer verdict into the Manager's gate decision or permit repair on integration branches.

## Evidence

- New master-exit-only affirmative completeness detail, retaining the blocking consequence. [1]
