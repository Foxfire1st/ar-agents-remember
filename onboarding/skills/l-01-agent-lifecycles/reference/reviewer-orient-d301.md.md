## Purpose

Orient over every changed file, including unattributed changes. For knowledge changes inspect before/after, family interactions and unchanged sibling realizations; attribution adds a dimension and never filters the diff. Supplied Curator producer fields remain unchanged for co-resolution. This reference is not semantic acceptance or a new source of producer data.

An omission repair lists and checks every behavior introduced by the diff. Card-date claims are checked against the adding commit, verdict times use a same-step clock read, and every-member findings use a whole-candidate scan rerun at verification.
