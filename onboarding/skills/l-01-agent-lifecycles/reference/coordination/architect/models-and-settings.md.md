## Purpose

The Architect's rules for selecting a seat model and its authorized settings. It grants no additional authority.

## Code Commentary

When a model the developer names is missing from a list, the Architect looks it up and checks the harness's and host's versions before asking about another model. The developer may name role sets (complete role settings for different situations); the Architect may switch between the sets he named on its own, for example when a provider is out, and keeps the earlier settings bytes, while any other change to his settings needs his word for that case. Every seat starts on the developer-named model or the role default; a variant is never chosen from its name.

## Evidence

- A missing named model is looked up and the harness and host versions are checked first. [1]
- Named role sets may be switched by the Architect; any other settings change needs the developer's word. [2]
- Seats start on the named model or role default, never a variant chosen by name. [3]
- The Architect role points here before selecting a seat model and its settings. [4]
