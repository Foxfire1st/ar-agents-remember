## Purpose

The Architect's rules for deciding or escalating a coordination issue. It grants no additional authority.

## Code Commentary

The Manager's standing rules are kept on one maintained page, not a ruling file per case. A formal process decision made within the Architect's authority is reported afterwards in one line. Existing rulings apply first, and unanswered questions about how the work is done are decided at the next check and reported briefly. A process dead end that recurs goes to the developer as a design question with the count, what the cases share and where the cure belongs. When review stays blocked by a larger problem after the Architect's ruling, no further review round or scope is granted and the problem goes to the developer. A process weakness seen mid-master is written down with signs for returning to it, and no process is rebuilt mid-master unless the work is stuck.

## Evidence

- One page of coordinating rules, one-line reports of formal decisions, and standing rulings applied first. [1]
- Recurring dead ends and unresolved blockers go to the developer without granting rounds or scope. [2]
- Mid-master process weaknesses are recorded, not rebuilt. [3]
- The Architect role points here before deciding or escalating a coordination issue. [4]
