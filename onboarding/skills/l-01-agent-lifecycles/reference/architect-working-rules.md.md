## Purpose

Collects the Architect’s standing practices for requirements, developer decisions, coordination status and measured product claims.

## Code Commentary

The page keeps approval and scope decisions at their named owner: unsettled directions are consolidated before forwarding, drafts stay in scratch, and requirements describe the existing structure and recorded callers before prescribing a replacement. Its measurement section assigns observation runs to the Architect, distinguishes model-specific outcomes from unknown infrastructure trials, and treats the loop as an observation process rather than an automatic product gate.

## Evidence

- Requirement and design communication preserve explicit developer authority and draft custody. [1]
- Structure, evidence and native harness behavior are checked before new prescriptions. [2]
- The measurement section defines run ownership, repeat conditions, outcome reporting and token accounting. [3]
