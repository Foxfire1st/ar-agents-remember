## Purpose

Collects standing practices for measured evidence, serving-build comparison and sandbox or host boundaries.

## Code Commentary

Counts and recorded times come from producing calls and contemporaneous clock reads. Sandbox versions, harness settings, model selection and graphical measurements retain their measured setup. The page protects user-owned harness configuration and limits process or live-host changes to checked identities and previously allowed steps.

## Evidence

- Times, counts and tool mismatches use producing evidence and the serving build. [1]
- Sandbox setup and measurement claims retain their stated identity and user-setting boundaries. [2]
- Process signals and live-host operations remain bounded by checked identity and existing authority. [3]
