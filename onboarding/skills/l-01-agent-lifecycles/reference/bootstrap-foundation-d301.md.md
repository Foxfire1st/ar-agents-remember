## Purpose

This is the handover detail read at Bootstrap step 8, not at capsule intake, and its step label in the composition manifest names step 8. It separates authored knowledge from cards and baseline. Bootstrap reads the state from `memory_init`'s `knowledge` block — `not-recorded`, `recorded` at an exact identity, `unusable`, or a refused admission with its own next action — reports which one it is, and tells the user the next step in words they can act on: start the Architect, which organises memory and knowledge bootstrapping as a regular task, master or sprint according to the size of the work, through `c-14`'s Curator route. It does not reach the foundation and does not produce it.

Nothing about that foundation is fabricated to make a step run: no development leaf, worktree or enclosure is created for it, `task_doc` and `role_start` are not called by this seat, and no record is written by hand. A repository whose memory has a commit may still show `not-recorded` when the seat finishes; the report says exactly that rather than claiming the foundation happened. The read still happens after the baseline commit on a fresh root, and only memory that already has a commit may reach its foundation before onboarding or adoption.
