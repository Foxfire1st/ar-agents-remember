## Purpose

This is an application of the shared profile, not another language standard. Bind a walkthrough to the exact candidate and invariant revision/family guarantee, preserving terms, exact identities, actor, trigger and connected conditions/exclusions. A diagram must not hide a condition in an unlinked legend.

Separate promise, realization and proof; arrows establish no test result. Cite real evidence with limitations and distinguish missing, unexamined, unavailable and measured absence. A family visualization explains its authored joint guarantee, not a grouping-inferred promise.

Keep exact recorded text accessible by an explicit link and independently compare paraphrases/cases. Ambiguous explanation stays unaccepted until corrected/classified through the normal route; never rewrite frozen history to match art. Optional visuals supplement the report, never schema/admission/invariant checks, independent review or acceptance.
