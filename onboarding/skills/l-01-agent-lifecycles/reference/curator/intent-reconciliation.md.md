## Purpose

The Curator's rules for reconciling current intent, ruled change intent and implemented reality; role admission and handover authority stay in the curation operation.

## Code Commentary

Three bodies of evidence are compared: current intent (source, tests, onboarding contracts, entity boundaries, durable incident lessons), ruled change intent (task, developer decisions, approved design notes, Worker report, Reviewer verdict) and implemented reality (the complete supplied change set and its verification). The pass succeeds when they agree or every material divergence has reached its answering seat through the curation operation's failure and handover routes. For each affected contract the Curator states whether the implementation preserves, extends, deliberately supersedes or contradicts existing intent, and why the task's recorded authority permits that. Passing tests establish selected executable behaviour only, not coherent ownership, non-goals, negative knowledge or the separation between agent reasoning and control-plane state. A historical oddity becomes a permanent invariant only after its causal applicability and reconsideration condition are checked; the curation operation points to this file at that step.
