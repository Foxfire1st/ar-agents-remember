## Purpose

The Curator's rules for deciding each card sentence before editing a card or its paired notes, so cards state only what is true now.

## Code Commentary

Every sentence of a card and its paired notes, including reference captions and dated sections, is decided one by one; sentences are edited individually and a section is never replaced nor a card regenerated wholesale. A sentence true now keeps its wording, including one true about a retired thing, after verification in the supplied source; narrowing or dropping a sentence requires searching source and memory for its support first. A sentence about what happens is true only if that code runs today, and one saying something is written, started, sent or served is checked where the action starts, not only where its path or setting is defined.

The decision list (keep, reword or drop, with the deciding source line) is written before editing and goes into the report; a reworded sentence is a new claim and names no receiver, helper or step the source lacks. False claims are repaired or withdrawn in every current carrier found by a bounded search (card body, paired notes, sibling cards), as a class. Past accounts, including dated change notes, are removed from the card and named in the card's history row, while their sentences that are true now stay without the date or change story.

## Evidence

- Each sentence is decided individually; true sentences keep their wording; a sentence about what happens needs code that runs today. [1]
- The decision list is written before editing and reported; reworded sentences are verified as new claims. [2]
- False claims are repaired across all carriers, and past accounts move to the history row. [3]
- The Curator role points here before editing a card or its paired notes. [4]
