## Purpose

Canonical reference account of four job lenses—bug, feature, triage and research—for scoping seats. A dispatched role receives its flavor in its brief rather than choosing another lens. A lens changes the opening move, retrieval lean and decide default, not authority or phase gates. The phase spine described here belongs to the retained scoping account, not to every native role capsule.

## Code Commentary

### Logic And Boundaries

The table and four job sections pair each lens with an opening move, retrieval lean and nonautomatic decide default. Bug leads with reproduction and proven root cause, leaning on Relationship and Intent. Feature fixes intent, scope and explicit non-goals, then uses design doctrine and Intent. Triage assesses severity, blast radius and ownership through a breadth scan; research states its question and leans on Semantics and onboarding.

Bug and feature usually decide to build. Triage commonly exits with a recommendation, routed owner or spawned job, but its source expressly allows escalation to a build when triage is the cheapest place to fix the problem. Research exits research-only by design and may recommend a follow-up build without performing it. Thus the older combined triage/research sentence must not erase triage's exception or confer a build path on research.

The source retains the Semantics and Relationship labels in its job table; the bug section uses native search from an anchor symbol to inspect callers, callees and impact, with Intent confirming the violated invariant. It also retains tasks/AGENTS.md design guidance and a feature-size choice between a w-02 artifact and a master/sub-task series. Those reference-account choices do not establish that a light-task API is currently writable or that the old orchestrator spine is injected into native role capsules. The canonical router says a role capsule is the selected role plus one applicable operation; the manifest marks lenses non-injected. A lens is a scoping hint, not a gate or authority grant.
