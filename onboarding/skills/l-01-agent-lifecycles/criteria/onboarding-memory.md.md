## Purpose

Standing and exploratory memory-review criteria for an adversarial non-author second reader. The catalog distinguishes a baseline review from sealed fix-verification and binds converted-memory fact-check/admission obligations through their requirements.

## Code Commentary

OM-1 checks every current body claim against actual shipped code, including removed or renamed behavior. OM-2 replaces legacy Update History review with independent statement-by-statement fact-check of every changed card, sidecar and record against final code, every history disposition/effect/reason against the diff, and important omissions. A blocking finding is repaired before closeout. The reviewer must have written none of those cards or records; read-only helpers do not supply that independent role acceptance.

The ordinary new onboarding contract is source-bound and row-only. This leaf's own changed-card rollout exception is in amended R53 rule 1; the catalog's general wording does not authorize writing updated through an unsupported producer or mislabeling changed prose no_impact.

OM-4 evaluates plausibility of admission justifications beyond structural presence and two checkable criteria: copied leaf acceptance criteria or a choice without real alternatives is not durable knowledge. OM-3 remains a candidate for legacy history ordering. Novel lenses and the promotion ratchet belong only to baseline review; fix-verification cannot enlarge its sealed finding set. These are lifecycle review duties, not newly enforced closeout predicates or fresh execution evidence.
