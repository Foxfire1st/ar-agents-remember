## Purpose

Standing and exploratory memory-review criteria for an adversarial non-author second reader. The catalog distinguishes a baseline review from sealed fix-verification and binds converted-memory fact-check/admission obligations through their requirements.

## Code Commentary

OM-1 checks every current body claim against actual shipped code, including removed or renamed behavior, and checks that historical accounts sit in history rows rather than cards. OM-2 is an independent statement-by-statement fact-check of every changed card, sidecar and record against final code, every history disposition/effect/reason against the diff, and important omissions. A blocking finding is repaired before closeout. The reviewer must have written none of those cards or records; read-only helpers do not supply that independent role acceptance.

The ordinary new onboarding contract is source-bound and row-only.

OM-4 evaluates plausibility of admission justifications beyond structural presence and two checkable criteria: copied leaf acceptance criteria or a choice without real alternatives is not durable knowledge. OM-3 is standing: every open worklist item has its required content-bound history row, including owed family rows, while OM-2 judges each row's meaning. OM-2, OM-4 and OM-5 apply only to converted, text-format memory, and only a developer ruling may demote them. Novel lenses and the promotion ratchet follow the shared catalog review rules in `criteria/code-seam.md` and belong only to baseline review; fix-verification cannot enlarge its sealed finding set. These are lifecycle review duties, not newly enforced closeout predicates or fresh execution evidence.

OM-1 also checks affected paired notes and sibling cards found by a bounded search, including dated sections. Repair or withdraw the encountered false-claim class on every such current carrier and preserve true present-tense statements about retired things. The independent second reader still checks statements, history meaning and important omissions before closeout.

## Current account — OM-5, what a re-point and a reconsidered revision judge

OM-5 asks the reviewer to judge a `repoint` as a judgment: the new target is the code the alternative's `reconsider_when` is about, and the row's reason says against that code why the condition is not met, with a reason that leaves the question open finding that the row should have been a `raise`. A reconsidered revision written after a raise is judged against the developer's recorded answer: the alternative's status matches it, a link re-pointed in `records` names the code the condition is about, and the `changed` row cites the answer.

- OM-5 makes each re-point and each reconsidered revision after a raise a judged obligation the reviewer checks against the code and the recorded answer. [5]

- Current body claims are checked against shipped code under OM-1. [1]

- The authored source owns this contract; the distribution copy is not a separate authority. [4]

- OM-1 also checks affected paired notes and sibling cards found by a bounded search, including dated sections. Repair or withdraw the encountered false-claim class on every such current carrier and keep true statements about the current state. The independent second reader still checks statements, history meaning and important omissions before closeout. [6]
