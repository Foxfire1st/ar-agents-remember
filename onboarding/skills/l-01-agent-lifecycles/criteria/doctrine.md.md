## Code Commentary

### Logic And Boundaries

The standing list is D-1 through D-6. D-1 requires a real file-and-mechanism anchor for a claim that doctrine enforces or automatically refuses something. D-2 sweeps sibling doctrine for contradictions; D-3 walks waits, gates and handovers as the obeying agent and asks who can unblock each state. The L8 deadlock and inert-flag accounts are catching history, not evidence that a present operation ran.

D-4 distinguishes organizational masters, whose leaves land directly on super, from atomic masters with an intermediate integration branch. It rejects feature/fix work on main, super or atomic integration branches, while retaining the narrow conflict-resolution and plane-owned ref-movement exception. D-5 separates detected graph/readiness facts and stable tie-breaking from role-owned dependency, execution-nature, priority and reprioritization judgments.

D-6 permits bounded durable requirement/step identifiers in comments while excluding task, chat, review, decision-item and report provenance. Its 83-comment-lines/22-modules figure is explicitly the historical 260815-DAG-L15 observation; it is not a current source census.

Baseline review runs the standing floor and the brief's novel lenses (default two). Fix-verification uses the sealed evidence for outstanding IDs and does not recensus criteria or create a finding outside the sealed list. The promotion ratchet requires separate first-review catches and loop-owner acceptance; default five dry engagements demotes a standing criterion to spot-check. Mechanizable criteria graduate into scoped diagnostics. The catalog is a review source, not permission to execute a review or a transaction gate.
