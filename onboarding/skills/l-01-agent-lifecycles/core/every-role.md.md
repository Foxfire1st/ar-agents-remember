## Purpose

Carries the shared standing duties delivered beside a role capsule at launch. Role-qualified lines keep the named audience; unqualified lines state shared working boundaries.

## Code Commentary

The source states compact duties, grouped under headings for role and authority, role starts, questions and scope, parallel work and waiting, requirements and limits, repository instructions, evidence and handovers, and attempts and repair records; it carries no unit IDs, and `core/digest-map.json` maps each of its places to the core-file units it draws on. It also states, for every role, the short shared rules on removing scratch paths, stopping a process or acting on the live host, counting from the producing call, comparing builds before calling a mismatch a defect, and naming the sandbox versions and the graphics setup behind a measurement. Its presence is distinct from composing the larger core doctrine into the role instruction stream. Delivery selection and placement belong to composition-manifest.json; the digest map records source-unit custody.
