## Purpose

Carries the shared standing duties delivered beside a role capsule at launch. Role-qualified lines keep the named audience; unqualified lines state shared working boundaries.

## Code Commentary

The source states compact duties (342 words), grouped under four headings: role and authority, parallel work and waiting, requirements and limits, and evidence and handovers; it carries no unit IDs, and `core/digest-map.json` maps each of its places to the core-file units it draws on. It also states, for every role, the short shared rules on calling AR tools only on `agents-remember-task`, running Git in code/memory roots, removing scratch paths, stopping a process or acting on the live host, refusing unapproved limits, and checking the full population (base-failure proof for pre-existing failures lives in `core/invariants.md` and `criteria/report-verification.md`); Architect and Reviewer lines bind capsule ceilings to `WIRE_TOKEN_CEILINGS` and `COMPILED_CAPSULE_TOKEN_CEILINGS` in `wire_budget.py`. Its presence is distinct from composing the larger core doctrine into the role instruction stream. Delivery selection and placement belong to composition-manifest.json; the digest map records source-unit custody.

For Architect, Orchestrator and Manager, the digest states scope-based `retire_child` authority (Architects cover all scopes, Managers cover their master and leaves, Orchestrators cover their sprint, masters and leaves) and developer delegation for pushes, PRs, releases and `--no-verify`.
