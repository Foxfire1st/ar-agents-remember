## Purpose

The canonical minimal frame distinguishes task worktree location records and the landing chain's retained contract lifecycle from structural seat binding. ## Contract and ownership

`worktree_start` creates the task worktree and records its location; `worktree_attach` resolves the contract and reports that worktree. The landing chain records completion or abandonment in its contract. There is no ambient session-adoption, outside-lifecycle dropped-call or system-owned paused rule in the corrected frame.

`role_start` binds a seat to its canonical document and role. The Architect starts without a document; other seats bind to their sprint, master, leaf or exact review seam. Only the owning seat uses the machinery its own role file assigns. The Architect/leaf-Curator sentences remain the propagation witness for INV-CQCPAD.

## Trust checkpoint and retained safety boundaries

The frame assigns the opening trust checkpoint to Architect and Orchestrator where their role file binds it. Started roles consume supplied task facts and workspace bindings instead of repeating it. The named context_packet request includes drift and freshness, and the account reports repository, memory roots and ancestry before relying on memory. Committed-source drift maintenance is approval-gated up the chain; dirty-source drift is work in progress, not a maintenance exception.

Provider-start/degradation instructions are retained source residue, not authority to restore retired setup/start/index/watch operations or removed arguments. Preserve their report/order/no-kill boundary: a degradation event holds provider starts, not every seat, and leaf-altitude seats receive no provider teardown authority. This instruction evidence is not current runtime execution or acceptance.
