## Governing Overview

[tests overview](overview.md)

## Purpose

A master's series refusals name the master, the contract cell or folder, and the repair. The cases cover a series branch that is gone, checkpoint and memory capture against an unresolved series branch, and a dirty integration worktree: the dirty atomic-master refusal guides delivery through a closed leaf and owner publication or discard and advertises no integration-workbench commit.

## Code Commentary

`_landed_master` builds the landed master fixture and `_names` checks that a refusal names the contract, cell and repair. `test_dirty_integration_worktree_names_closed_leaf_publication_or_discard` carries the ruled wording; the archive cases name the directory and the action.

## Evidence

- The dirty atomic-master refusal guides delivery through a closed leaf and owner publication or discard, and advertises no integration-workbench commit. [1]
- A series refusal names the master, the contract cell or folder, and the repair. [2]
