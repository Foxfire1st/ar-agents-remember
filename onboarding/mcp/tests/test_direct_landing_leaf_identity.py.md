## Purpose

Protect explicit direct-landing leaf identity, closed history creation and kept closing-receipt recovery.

## Code Commentary

The grant-bound landing judges its admitted leaf before an optional caller leaf_id. Omitting that hint still reaches the invariant gate: the refusal test requires the bound leaf identity and the actual finding count (four in its regression fixture). A bogus hint cannot replace that owner; when a foreign leaf's history is open, the public refusal names the bound and foreign leaves and retains the leaf_id hint. The lower-level history-selector cases separately protect explicit valid ownership, rowless closed-history creation and invalid/foreign-owner refusal.

Recovery proof has two complementary scopes: the public lifecycle tool now admits the interrupted doorless direct generation, preserves its operation key/counter, completes it and clears its retained receipt; the real executor witness independently exercises receipt settlement. The turned public test keeps the original interruption vehicle, rather than substituting an executor-only proof for public admission.

The former public-control refusal assertion is historical and superseded by MIK-R143, not evidence that canonical bound generations currently refuse recovery. That owner transition is separate from L161's two refusal-text repairs. The current public witness stops execution at admission; it does not itself prove a post-code-leg interruption.
