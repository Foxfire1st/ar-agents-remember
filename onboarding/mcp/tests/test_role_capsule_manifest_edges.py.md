## Purpose

Manifest/source-admission edge cases plus production audience/digest regressions.

## Code Commentary

Owns six whole edge tests covering role/skill lookup, specialization shape/blank identity, absent override winner, invalid root, missing selected manifest and composing-root boundaries. Two compile_launch_capsule cases reject malformed recipient markers after common and excluded passages with exact status/line/no-delivery, and prove equal source bytes with different accepted audience projections produce different streams/digests. Restoring projection is deterministic; unrelated Curator source and diagnostic purpose metadata stay inert.

The shared-digest cases read `core/digest-map.json` against `core/every-role.md`: every digest place, clause and unit has exactly one home with matching line hashes, headings and role-prefix audiences, retired core units must carry the exact recorded retirement decision and no live binding, and lost, overlapping, unknown or ambiguous bindings fail on mutants. For every shipped role and operation the every-role digest is delivered once as the shared block outside the capsule instructions, carries the round-archive and evidence-catalog rules once for the Worker, Reviewer and Curator, and a one-byte change to it moves the semantic digest without changing the rendered instructions. The Git-root scope rule has one source unit and one digest place addressed to every role. The Reviewer's complete-diff rule is read from the real tree: it stands inline once in its role file and not in the knowledge-use detail. The two moved-piece witnesses, for the Architect's template pointers and the Curator's inline workflow paragraphs, live in `test_role_capsule_moved_piece_reach.py`.

## Evidence Boundaries

- The digest case independently holds source bytes fixed while changing the admitted projection.
- No actor launch or vendor call occurs.
- These edge tests are not duplicated in the admission module.
- Finite malformed examples are not a universal language/semantic validator.

This card describes assertion/support/document source, not a test invocation, independent verdict or publication.

Real-corpus cases bind the Investigator's instruction-shortfall review, the Architect's and Manager's third-rejected-round progress judgment and the Architect's Curator-measurement detail to their declared source, roles and step and to the exact read action that links them. The frozen core-unit map checks each range digest and exact non-heading line coverage. Inherited detail audiences retain source and undeclared-file negative controls. These are source assertions/support behavior, not a fresh invocation, host result or AR acceptance.
