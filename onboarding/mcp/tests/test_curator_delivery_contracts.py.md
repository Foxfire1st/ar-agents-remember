## Purpose

Proves that the Curator's curation doctrine stands in its source owner files and actually reaches the Curator at the right action: in the role text, in the step details delivered with the compiled capsule, and through the links of the emitted launch files.

## Code Commentary

The module-scoped `deliveries` fixture compiles every role/operation pair the lifecycle manifest declares (`compile_matrix`), so no pair is sampled. The clause tables and checkers come from `agents_remember_test_support.testing.curator_contracts`; the owner files are the Curator role, the curation operation, the onboarding-memory criteria and the curator and knowledge references (card-sentence decisions, card formats and citations, serving-build writer, publication report, current prose and history, change routing, knowledge commands).

- **Role.** The Curator role keeps its current-truth, continuation and healing clauses, and links the curation operation's complete-checks section, whose clauses are required and fail when deleted.
- **Step details.** `DETAIL_ACTIONS` names each detail, the action sentence that points to it and the roles that carry it; over the whole matrix a detail is delivered, with byte-identical content and a non-empty step, exactly when the role carries its action pointer, and the Curator's emitted launch files resolve those links.
- **Delivered content.** The card-sentence detail carries current-truth and all-carrier repair, the card-format detail carries every invariant disposition definition, the writer detail binds the serving build's identity and the publication detail its report semantics, and the history detail names that a deleted row retiring an invariant names only `retire`.
- **Writer examples.** `writer_findings` flags a bare `agents-remember` writer command in a table or shell example but not the serving-bound `-P -m agents_remember.cli` form or prose; no Curator capsule block, detail or followed skill carries a bare-path writer example.
- **Healing.** No curation doctrine surface limits healing to one's own faults or routes all encountered debt away, and exactly one complete encountered-memory healing paragraph stands in the role's `Repair and finish the stage` section and in each Curator capsule's role block, compiled blocks and emitted launch file, and none in any other role's.
- **Card maintenance and fact-check.** The role holds one card-scope and one entity-catalog pointer that resolve from source, compiled origins and the actual emitted launch file; semantic-truth, line-count and independent fact-check duties keep their own homes in the card detail, operation and criteria.
- **Tool failure.** A failed memory tool leaves independent work on other admitted memory tools while existing refusal stops still bind dependent work.
