## Purpose

Check compact task-document write answers, by-file/diff custody and removed-operation fallback on controlled fixtures.

## Code Commentary

Four ordinary write fixtures combine recursive location-free targets, distinct-location counts, full-answer bound and independently captured whole-detail identity. Other operations have precise phrase, by-file digest/conflict, binding recovery and dry-diff cases; they do not inherit a measured target simply because they share the tool.

Removed-read fixtures exercise valid, missing and corrupt content returning operation-removed. Their outcome assertions do not prohibit the helper's best-effort identity parsing. Attach/detach dry fixtures separate the complete caller diff report from the investigation detail: losing the latter must not erase an independently retained diff or cause a false retained-detail promise.

These are fixture assertions, not a whole real landing, an unbounded resource witness, or task acceptance.
