## Purpose

The evidence packet's contract cases: comparison identity, effective converted-base reads, measured-empty versus absent, bounded reads at two sizes, relationship owners, supplemental receipts, claim authority, the prepared-address seam and the measured read bounds.

## Code Commentary

The module covers MIK-R102 rules 1-8, 11 and 12 through the shipped owners: a foreign repository with equal trees and a different conversion are refused, an existing empty file is `measured-empty`, a range past the decoded prefix is truncated with a progressing continuation, a relationship walk follows the owner's own field, a changed supplemental source answers a successor receipt, and a present label on an absent basis stays unresolved. Its `world` fixture builds the comparison through the landed owners, so the tests do not invent side states.

## Evidence

- The packet contract cases read through the landed owners rather than fabricated side states. [1]
- A foreign repository with equal trees is never this comparison. [2]
- A literal continuation crosses a separator and a long line without certifying absence. [3]
