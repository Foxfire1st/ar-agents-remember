# Message-file doctrine oracle

[Governing tests overview](overview.md)

## Contract

The oracle derives ten retained skill surfaces from the product inventory, checks required file-message markers across roles, operations and templates, and rejects pasted evidence/verdict directives or pinned numeric message limits. The implementation operation's own marker check reads the text its Worker actually receives — that operation file plus `roles/worker.md` — because the messaging passage lives once, in the Worker role the operation is delivered with. Every other surface file's markers are read from its own text, with two exceptions that already stood and that this leaf did not touch: `operations/curation.md` is compiled as the Curator's and the Manager's addressed capsules and checked there, and `roles/curator.md`'s `body`/`attach` markers may be satisfied by the Curator's workflow-1 message detail (test lines 261-292). The forbidden-directive scan reads each surface file's own lines and also the lines of that message detail and of both compiled curation capsules, wherever they carry text. Legal/illegal-line controls qualify its regexes; compiled capsules exercise the shared producer without a parent. This is source inventory and compiled-text coverage, not live consumption by every harness or parented question-address routing.

## Current audience, delivery and evidence contract

The oracle now checks both Curator and Manager addressed curation capsules for every source surface. Only the Curator's `body`/`attach` facet may come from its exact delivered workflow-1 message detail; forbidden-message scans also inspect that file. Reviewer/Curator marker equivalents have per-facet deletion and cross-role negative controls, preserving separate untouched-role wording. No live transport or every-harness consumption is inferred.
