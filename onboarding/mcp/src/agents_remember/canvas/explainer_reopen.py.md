## Purpose

What an archive takes of a leaf's explainer, and why a reopen after it is refused.

## Code Commentary

A removal keeps the explainer's published revisions and the seat counter beside them, so a reopen starts a new seat at the next generation. An archive reclaims both with the task's frames, and its receipt names for each leaf the revisions and the counter it took and why a reopen is unavailable: a fresh counter would number seats into ids the archived chats still hold. A build reads that receipt and refuses with the reason it records.
