## Contract

DeveloperGrantRequest forbids extra fields and carries repository, canonical path and give/revoke action. The developer writer resolves a canonical subTask, proves unstartedness, preserves idempotent issuer/time and attempts exclusive recreation of the exact removed grant when work starts during revocation. FileExistsError preserves another giver's grant and OSError is suppressed; these branches establish neither exact-record restoration nor serial consistency and do not satisfy the unchanged INV-X16RQH promise. Started, ambiguous and wrong-kind cases refuse; the route returns the product's words and refreshes stored Markdown through the ordinary task store. It is not an agent-role grant API and adds no task-document field or shell-write protection.
