## Purpose

Derives a revision's standing from eligible history and its retained content in the selected memory tree.

Standing derivation also folds qualifying closed consolidation passes into the revision standing (`consolidated`), keeping qualifying-keep (`stands` or entry-only) consolidation passes distinct from ordinary review confirmation while requiring exact content/revision alignment.
