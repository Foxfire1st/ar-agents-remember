## Governing Overview

[Governing overview](../overview.md)

## Purpose

Preserves the governing history row for a leaf's changed invariant or family guarantee.

## Evidence

- Changed and retirement judgments keep authored changes visible. [1]

- A conflicting later invariant row cannot replace the governing change judgment. [2]

- A changed family guarantee requires a governing judgment that records the change. [3]
