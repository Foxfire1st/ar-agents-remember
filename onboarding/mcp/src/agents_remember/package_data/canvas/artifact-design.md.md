## Purpose

The product's default design text: it steers the look of a generated page — character, colour, type, spacing and the form of recurring parts — and cannot change what a page may read, write or start.

## Code Commentary

- The text opens with its own non-authority statement: it steers appearance and cannot weaken the page's isolation.
- It fixes a small palette, one type scale and an 8px rhythm, so pages from different attempts share a house style.
- A repository may replace it whole through `system/artifact-design.md`; the shipped text stays the default.
