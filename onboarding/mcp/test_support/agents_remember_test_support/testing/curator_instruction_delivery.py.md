# mcp/test_support/agents_remember_test_support/testing/curator_instruction_delivery.py

## Governing Overview

[overview](overview.md)

## Purpose

The module holds instruction-delivery assertions that `mcp/tests/test_curator_instruction_wording.py` calls from its test methods: coordination step timing, Reviewer baseline intake, the refused-write ladder and the Curator repository-foundation boundaries.

## Code Commentary

`assert_coordination_steps` checks, for the Architect (planning) and Orchestrator (coordination), that each scenario's answer arrives either in the compiled shared block (exactly once for the closeout-tree clause) or in the detail delivered behind its action pointer in seat-recovery or master-state. `assert_correct_write_ladder` requires the shared refused-write paragraph to link the served refused-write detail once, checks the ladder clauses and permission delivery in the served copy and in the Manager's delivered detail, keeps the detail placeholder out of the Architect and Orchestrator role files, and checks the Worker's hard stops. `assert_foundation_boundaries` checks required clauses in the Curator role's assignment and handover sections and in the repository-foundation reference. Each uses the shipped corpus or the compile callable the test supplies; the module starts no role and writes only temporary report paths.

## Evidence

- Each coordination scenario's answer is found in the compiled shared block or the delivered step detail. [1]
- The Reviewer intake is judged on instructions delivered with the baseline-review detail. [2]
- The refused-write ladder and permission rule reach the shared block, the served detail and the Manager. [3]
- The Curator role and foundation reference carry the foundation admission and output clauses. [4]
